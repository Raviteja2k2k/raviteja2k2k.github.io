Facilities and roles

Where the work happened

2010 – 2025
Founder & Chief Dental Surgeon, DV Dental Hospital & Implant Centre. A multi-specialty dental hospital at about 30 patients a day, with 26 staff. Full digital workflow: CAD/CAM, intraoral scanning, digital radiography and CBCT-to-3D-printed surgical models. Kept open through COVID-19 as an emergency referral hub. Closed in April 2025 to focus on health tech.
2010 – now
Senior Administrator, Manjary Nursing Home. A multi-specialty nursing home with 600+ patient encounters a month. Clinical governance, infection control, audits and compliance.
2007 – now
Founder, Manjary Dental Care Centre. A technology-augmented clinic with EMR workflows, automated recalls and an in-house 3D-printing lab; I wrote its full SOP framework.
2019 – now
Founder & Lead Engineer, a clinical 3D-printing studio. It turns CT and CBCT (DICOM) scans into patient-specific surgical models, surgical guides and patient-education models.
2013 – 2019
Founder & CEO, Shree Manjary Medical Agency. Medical and dental supplies distribution, grown from zero to 30+ pharmacy and clinic clients; clean founder exit.
2006 – 2010
Associate Dentist → Branch Head → Regional Clinical Administrator, Lavanya Dental Hospital Group. Branch Head within 18 months; set up 2 new branches, each operational within 90 days; built the group's 9-branch financial accountability framework.
Governance and outcomes

Protocols, audits and measured results

  • Spore-tested sterilisation governance at DV Dental Hospital that cut reinfection by 90%.
  • EMR with appointment automation and documentation compliance above 95%.
  • An antibiotic-rationality audit for rotating junior clinicians: inappropriate use down 40%.
  • At Manjary Nursing Home: a sepsis-care protocol that shortened length of stay, discharge cycle time cut 25%, monthly hygiene audits with PPE compliance above 95%, and patient satisfaction up about 30%.
  • An SOP framework at Manjary Dental Care Centre (hand hygiene, sterilisation, biomedical waste, PPE, environmental cleaning) with no major audit non-conformities since 2015.
  • An EMR-driven recall programme: 100+ recall visits a month and patient retention above 85%.
